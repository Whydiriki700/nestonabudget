About Nest on a Budget

Nest on a Budget is a small independent site about living well in small rented homes. We write for apartment renters who cannot drill, cannot renovate, and do not have a furniture-store budget - because that is most renters.

Every idea we publish is checked against three rules: it must be renter-friendly (no permanent changes), it must fit a genuinely small space, and its cost must be stated honestly in the article. Prices we mention are typical retail ranges at the time of writing, not promises.

Our articles are written and edited by people, with research and drafting help from AI tools. When an image on this site or on our Pinterest pins is AI-generated, we mark it with Pinterest's AI-modified setting when publishing the pin.

Questions or corrections: see the Contact page.
